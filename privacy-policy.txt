SCP FIELD OPERATIONS - PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 28 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the game SCP Field Operations
(the "Game") uses, why, who it is shared with, and what rights you have.

1. WHO WE ARE

The Game is published by Czikai Máté Gábor, a private individual, under the
name "DMS Project" ("we", "us"). We are the data controller for the
information described in this policy.

Contact (privacy questions and requests): rexhun999@yahoo.ro

2. THE SHORT VERSION

- We do not run our own servers and we do not keep a database about you.
- We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
- Multiplayer runs through Steam. One player hosts the session and the others
  connect to them.
- Voice chat is sent live to the other players in your session. It is not
  recorded or saved by us.
- If you use the Discord features, Discord receives the information described
  in section 3.4.

3. WHAT INFORMATION IS USED AND WHY

3.1 Steam
The Game uses Steamworks (Valve Corporation) for the Steam overlay,
friends, lobbies, invites and multiplayer networking. The Game uses your
Steam ID and your Steam name to:
- show your name to the other players in your session (for example above
  your character),
- create and join multiplayer lobbies and send game data between players.
Multiplayer traffic uses Steam's networking. When you join through Steam,
your connection normally goes through Valve's relay network, so other
players do not see your IP address. If you join a session directly by IP
address or on a local network, the host can see your IP address.
How Valve handles your data is described in Valve's privacy policy:
https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/

3.2 Gameplay data shared with other players
While you play together, the Game sends the information needed to play to
the other players in your session: for example your character's position,
movement, actions, items, health, and your in-game settings that affect
what others see (such as your selected character). This data exists only
for the duration of the session and is not stored by us.

3.3 Voice chat
If voice chat is enabled, the Game uses your microphone:
- Only while voice chat is on (open mic, or while you hold the push-to-talk
  key), and never while the microphone is switched off in the settings.
- Echo cancellation and noise reduction run on your own computer.
- Your voice is compressed and sent live to the other players in your
  session (through the session host). Other players hear it as part of the
  game, for example from your character's position or through in-game
  radios.
- We do not record, store or analyse voice chat.
- In-game monsters can react to how loud you are. This is calculated on the
  computers in the session and is not stored.
- Some game mechanics (for example a monster that imitates voices) may keep
  short snippets of voice audio in memory during a match, and may play them
  back to the players in that match. These snippets are discarded when the
  match ends and are never saved to disk or sent to us. You can turn this
  off in the settings ("MIMIC MAY USE MY VOICE").
You can switch voice chat or your microphone off in the settings at any
time.

3.4 Discord (optional)
If Discord is running on your computer, or you choose to connect your
Discord account, the Game uses the Discord Social SDK (Discord Inc.) for:
- Rich Presence: Discord shows your friends that you are playing the Game
  and what you are doing in it (for example "In the van" or "On a mission").
- Friends and invites: you can see your Discord friends in the Game and
  invite them to your session, or join a session they invited you to.
For this the Game receives from Discord your Discord user ID, username,
display name and profile picture, your Discord friends list and their
online status, and sends your game activity to Discord. This information is
used only inside the Game while it runs. We do not store it.
You can disconnect the Game from your Discord account at any time in
Discord's settings (User Settings > Authorized Apps), and you can turn off
activity sharing in Discord's settings.
How Discord handles your data is described in Discord's privacy policy:
https://discord.com/privacy

3.5 Music you share in the game
The Game lets you play your own music files from a folder on your computer
with an in-game item. When you play a song, that file is sent to the other
players in your session so they can hear it, and it is kept in a temporary
cache on their computers. Only share files you are allowed to share (see
the Terms of Service).

3.6 Information stored on your own computer
The Game stores your settings (graphics, audio, controls, key bindings,
voice settings, selected character), your game progress, and log files on
your own computer. This data stays on your computer and is not sent to us.
Some of it (for example your settings or Steam name in a log file) may
include personal information, and you can delete it at any time by deleting
the Game's data folders.

4. LEGAL BASIS (EU / EEA)

We use the information above:
- to provide the Game and its multiplayer features you choose to use
  (performance of our agreement with you, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR),
- for voice chat and the Discord features, based on your choice to use
  them, which you can withdraw at any time by turning them off (consent,
  Article 6(1)(a) GDPR),
- to keep the Game working and secure, for example to prevent cheating or
  abuse in a session (legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

- Other players in your multiplayer session, as described in section 3.
- Valve Corporation (Steam), as the platform the Game runs on.
- Discord Inc., only if you use the Discord features.
We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else.
Valve and Discord are based in the United States, so information they
process may be transferred outside the EU/EEA under their own safeguards.

6. HOW LONG INFORMATION IS KEPT

We do not keep any of the information described in this policy on our
side. Session data, voice and shared music exist only while the session
runs (shared music may remain in the other players' temporary cache until
it is cleared). Data on your computer stays until you delete it. Valve and
Discord keep data according to their own policies.

7. CHILDREN

The Game is not intended for children under 13, and you must be at least 13
years old to play. If you are under 16 and live in the EU/EEA, ask a parent
or guardian before using voice chat or connecting your Discord account. If
you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us
and we will help remove it.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

If you live in the EU/EEA, you have the right to ask for access to,
correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to
its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because
we do not store data about you, in most cases there will be nothing for us
to hand over or delete, but you can always contact us at
rexhun999@yahoo.ro and we will answer within one month.
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection
authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for
Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP), https://www.dataprotection.ro/ - or the
authority of the EU country where you live.

9. SECURITY

Multiplayer traffic through Steam is encrypted by Steam's networking. Keep
in mind that other players in your session receive the information listed
in section 3, so only play and talk with people you trust.

10. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

If the Game starts using information in a new way (for example automatic
crash or bug reports), we will update this policy before that change is
released and change the "Last updated" date above. The latest version is
always available at the address where you found this policy.
